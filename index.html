SURVIVAL FIELD NOTES

How to hunt

Move on all fours

Use the left side of the screen as a joystick. On desktop, use WASD. Hold RUN to sprint — it burns life faster.

Paw & bite

PAW is fast and knocks animals back. BITE is slower, hits harder, and devours small prey in one lunge. Keyboard: J and K.

Feed the hunger

Your life drains constantly. Kill prey, stand over the body, and devour it to refill life. The less you eat, the faster you die.

Day & night

Every wood turns from day to dusk to night. Night is deadlier: predators are bolder and hunger bites harder. Watch the clock.

Tilt to steer

Enable Tilt steer in the pause menu to lean into turns. On iPhone and iPad, motion permission is requested the first time you enable it.

500 woods

Each level is a freshly generated wood with its own biome, weather and time of day. Devour enough prey and fell enough predators to clear it.

Drag the right side of the screen to look around. Your progress saves automatically.
