Field notes

How to Hunt

01

Run the wood

Use the left thumbstick or WASD. On desktop, drag the mouse to look. Tilt controls can be enabled in settings.

02

Paw & bite

Tap PAW to swipe in a wide arc. Tap BITE for a close, heavy snap. Enemies wind up before they strike — sidestep or strike first.

03

Feed to live

Your vitality constantly fades. Down a rabbit or beast, stand over it and hold EAT. The more you eat, the longer you last.

04

Find the gate

Complete the hunt and the Moon Gate wakes. Follow the compass, step through, and meet a new wood. There are 500.
